# Code of conduct

These are teaching repositories. People arrive at them precisely because they do
not yet know the thing being taught, which sets the standard for how they get
treated.

## Expected

- Assume the person asking has less context than you, and answer accordingly
- Criticise code, decisions and claims. Not people
- If someone is wrong about how something works, show them, ideally with a
  reproduction

## Not accepted

- Condescension towards anyone learning. "Just read the docs" is not an answer
- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind
- Deliberately wasting maintainer time

## Enforcement

Report anything to nyabangasamson@gmail.com. Reports stay private. Depending on
severity the response ranges from a warning to a permanent block, and the
maintainer's decision is final.
